DESCUBRE LA REALIDAD
LA VERDAD SOBRE ESENCIA
01
LIE "ESENCIA IS STEALING PUBLIC LAND / TAKING CABO ROJO"

Every acre was purchased from private landowners. No public land involved. No residents displaced because nobody was living on the site.

02
LIE "ESENCIA IS A PRIVATE CITY / GATED ENCLAVE FOR OUTSIDERS"

Esencia is not a city. It's a low-density, 2,000-acre community with a town center, trails, and public beach amenities. And 60% of buyers are from Puerto Rico.

03
LIE "ESENCIA WILL PRIVATIZE THE BEACHES"

Puerto Rico law guarantees public beach access. Esencia cannot and will not change that. The plan adds 4 NEW public access points and new beach facilities, making the coast more accessible than it is today.

04
LIE "ESENCIA IS DESTROYING PRISTINE NATURE / FORESTS, MANGROVES, WETLANDS"

A higher-density development was already approved before Esencia existed. The new plan cuts the built footprint by 80%. More than 75% of the site stays open space. The plan includes 33+ acres of wetland and mangrove restoration.

05
LIE "THE LAND IS UNTOUCHED AND ESENCIA IS CAUSING ALL THE DAMAGE"

The land is not untouched. Years of abandoned construction and damaging uses left it degraded. Esencia has already removed 54,000+ tons of garbage from the property. Some images circulating online show damage caused by neighboring parties, not Esencia. A complaint was filed with DNER.

AGUA E INFRAESTRUCTURA
MENTIRA "ESENCIA WILL STEAL WATER FROM LOCAL PEOPLE"
Esencia does not rely on the public water system. The project is built to be completely self-sufficient on water: its own deep wells, 1M-GALLON storage tank, advanced filtration, rainwater harvesting, and full wastewater recycling for irrigation. This system is specifically designed to reduce pressure on public infrastructure, not add to it.
MENTIRA "ESENCIA WILL STEAL LOCAL ENERGY AND STRAIN THE GRID"
Esencia generates its own power. A 68MW SOLAR system, battery energy storage, and smart microgrid technology mean the project runs on its own renewable energy, not the public grid. Backup generators include advanced emissions controls.
